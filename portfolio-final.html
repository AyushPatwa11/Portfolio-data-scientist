About Me

Turning Data Into
Real Impact

I'm a Data Science and AI-focused developer currently in my first year at Rungta College of Engineering, Bhilai. I'm passionate about solving real-world problems through machine learning, NLP, and computer vision — and I've already shipped 4+ projects that prove it.

Beyond code, I run CodeKaar on YouTube — a channel where I break down data science, AI concepts, and programming for students and self-learners. It's my way of giving back and building a community around tech education in India.

I dream big in AI. My goal isn't just to write models — it's to build systems that genuinely change lives: from helping farmers diagnose crops to detecting misinformation at scale. I believe India's next wave of AI innovation will come from builders like us who understand both the data and the problem.

I actively compete in hackathons and won HackSprint 2026 with VeriGraph-AI. Every competition is a chance to ship something real under pressure — and I love that challenge.
