### System Overview

**KisanBuddy** is an end-to-end AI-powered Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to empower farmers with real-time decision intelligence:

- **Crop Disease Diagnosis**: Multilingual image analysis for instant detection of plant pathologies.
- **Weather Hazard Alerts**: Predictive risk warnings powered by Earth Engine and LSTM forecasting.
- **Market Price Intelligence**: Live commodity pricing analytics and trends.

### Key Architecture Highlights

1. **Agentic Planner–Executor–Validator**: Integrates Vertex AI, SWIN Transformers, and Earth Engine services to validate advice before presenting to farmers.
2. **Offline-First PWA**: Uses IndexedDB with AES-256 encryption and background synchronization for seamless usage in low-connectivity areas.
