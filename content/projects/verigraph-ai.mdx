### System Overview

**VeriGraph AI** tracks and verifies news claims and online propaganda across diverse global information channels:

- **Multi-Source Collection**: Ingests data from News RSS, Facebook, Telegram, GDELT, and CommonCrawl.
- **Propagation Analytics**: Real-time virality scoring, threat assessment, and amplification network tracking.
- **Verification Engine**: Confidence-based claim verification powered by transformer models.
